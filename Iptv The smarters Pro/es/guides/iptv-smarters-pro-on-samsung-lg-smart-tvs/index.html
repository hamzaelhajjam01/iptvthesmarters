Installing IPTV Smarters Pro on Samsung Smart TVs (running Tizen OS) or LG Smart TVs (running WebOS) differs fundamentally from Android devices. Because these Smart TVs do not run Android OS, you cannot sideload .apk files directly. You must download the app from the TV's official app store or adjust regional settings if the app is hidden in your country.

1. Changing Regional Location Settings to Reveal Hidden App

Due to regional broadcaster restrictions, Samsung and LG often restrict IPTV Smarters Pro from appearing in search results in specific countries (such as Italy, Spain, or Brazil). If searching "Smarters Pro" yields no results, changing your TV's service region to the United States or United Kingdom unlocks the full app catalog.

Samsung Tizen Regional Code Sequence

  1. Go to Settings → General → Reset to initiate location setup.
  2. When the screen reaches the Terms and Conditions agreement page, press this exact remote sequence: Mute → Volume Up → Channel Up → Mute.
  3. A secret country selection menu will display. Select United States or United Kingdom.
  4. Complete setup, open Samsung Apps, and search for IPTV Smarters Pro.
For LG WebOS TVs: Go to Settings → All Settings → General → System → Location → LG Services Country. Uncheck "Set Automatically", select United Kingdom or United States, and accept the reboot prompt. Open the LG Content Store to install the app.

2. Performance Limitations of Built-In TV Apps

While installing IPTV Smarters Pro directly on a Smart TV eliminates the need for an external streaming stick, built-in TV processors are notoriously underpowered compared to dedicated streaming hardware like a Firestick 4K Max or Apple TV.

  • RAM Constraints: Smart TVs typically feature 1GB of total RAM shared with the TV's operating system, leading to lag when parsing massive 20,000+ item playlists.
  • VPN Restrictions: Tizen OS and WebOS do not support native VPN applications, making it impossible to encrypt your IPTV traffic at the TV level to bypass ISP throttling.
Recommendation: If your Smart TV app suffers from constant buffering or app crashes while browsing large channel lists, connect an external Amazon Firestick 4K or Android TV box to your TV's HDMI port for vastly superior processing power and VPN support.

Preguntas Frecuentes

Can I use a USB flash drive to install Smarters on a Samsung TV?

No. Samsung Tizen OS does not support installing Android `.apk` files via USB. You must download the native app from the Samsung Apps store after updating regional settings.

Why is IPTV Smarters Pro lagging on my LG Smart TV?

Smart TVs feature underpowered processors compared to dedicated streaming sticks. Parsing large 20,000+ channel playlists overloads TV RAM. Connecting a Firestick 4K resolves the lag.

الأسئلة الشائعة

كل ما تحتاج لمعرفته حول الاشتراك كـ موزع IPTV.

01 What is an IPTV reseller?

An IPTV reseller buys wholesale credits from a provider (like us) and resells IPTV subscriptions to end customers under their own brand. You set your own retail prices, keep the margin, and never deal with server infrastructure — we handle that.

02 How does the IPTV credit system work?

1 credit = 1 month of service for 1 active connection. When you create a 3-month subscription for a client, it uses 3 credits from your balance. Credits never expire and only deplete when you actively create or renew a line — so there's zero risk of losing unused credits.

03 How much can I make as an IPTV reseller?

Your margins range from 55% (Starter) to 62%+ (Enterprise). Example: with our Business pack (100 credits at $289), if you sell 100 lines at $10/month, you earn $1,000/month. After your one-time credit cost, you net $711 in the first month and $1,000/month recurring after that. Use the profit calculator above to model your numbers.

04 Do IPTV reseller credits expire?

No. Credits never expire. They sit in your panel balance indefinitely until you choose to use them. This is our biggest differentiator — you can buy credits now and activate lines weeks or months later with zero penalty.

05 Is there a minimum order to become a reseller?

Our Starter Pack begins at just 30 credits for $99, making it very accessible to test the market. Most serious resellers start with our Business Pack (100 credits at $289) to have room to scale.

06 Can I create sub-resellers under my account?

Yes. Professional and Enterprise tier resellers can create sub-reseller accounts and assign them a credit balance. This lets you build a distribution network — sell credits to smaller resellers at a markup and let them manage their own clients.

07 Do you offer free IPTV trial lines for resellers?

Yes. All resellers get access to a 24-hour free trial line generator inside their panel. You can offer these to prospective clients to let them test the service quality before they pay — the most effective closing tool in the IPTV reseller business.

08 What panel do you use for resellers?

We use an Xtream Codes-compatible panel, the industry standard for IPTV reseller management. From your dashboard you can: create and manage client lines, set expiry dates, monitor real-time active connections, lock lines to specific MACs or IPs, and generate free trial lines instantly.

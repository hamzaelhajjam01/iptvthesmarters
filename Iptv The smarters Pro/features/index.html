Common Questions

Feature & Setup FAQs

Everything you need to know about streaming quality, speed requirements, and hardware compatibility.

01 How reliable is the streaming quality?

We run on a 20 Gbps backbone edge network with adaptive bitrate delivery. This means the stream automatically adjusts to your internet connection to prevent buffering.

02 What internet speed do I need?

For smooth streaming, we recommend a minimum of 15 Mbps for Full HD (1080p) channels, and 25 Mbps for 4K Ultra HD streams.

03 How detailed is the EPG?

Our EPG (Electronic Program Guide) updates daily, offering comprehensive current and upcoming listings, and automatically adjusts to your local timezone.

04 What devices are compatible?

Almost everything with a screen. Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony, Android TV), Amazon Fire TV Stick, Apple TV, iOS/Android smartphones and tablets, Windows/Mac PCs, and dedicated set-top boxes like MAG and Formuler.

1. Correcting "Invisible Space" On-Screen Keyboard Typos

When entering credentials using an Amazon Firestick remote or mobile virtual keyboard, the auto-correct software routinely appends an invisible space character to the end of typed words or copy-pasted strings.

To an authentication API, "MyPassword1" and "MyPassword1 " are entirely different strings. The Xtream Codes authentication panel will instantly reject the string as an invalid login.

How to fix it: Highlight the Username, Password, and Server URL fields one by one. Move the cursor to the far right end of the text string and hit backspace once to confirm no invisible trailing space exists after the final character.

2. Clearing DNS Resolution Locks & Router Caches

If your login attempt stalls indefinitely on "Please Wait" without displaying an error message, your local router's DNS cache has likely stored a stale or blocked IP address for your provider's domain name.

When IPTV Smarters Pro attempts to resolve the domain name, the request times out at the router level before reaching the IPTV server.

  1. Power Cycle Your Router: Unplug your home router power cable for 60 seconds to flush its temporary DNS table.
  2. Modify Device DNS Settings: On your Firestick or Android TV, go to Network Settings → Configure IP, switch from DHCP to Static, and set DNS 1 to 8.8.8.8 (Google Public DNS) and DNS 2 to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare DNS).
  3. Clear App Cache: On your Firestick, navigate to Settings → Applications → Manage Installed Applications → IPTV Smarters Pro → Clear Cache.
Fastest Workaround: Connecting a VPN instantly redirects your device's network lookups to the VPN's secure DNS servers, completely bypassing local router DNS locks.

Veelgestelde Vragen

Why does the app stay stuck on 'Please Wait' indefinitely?

This indicates a DNS resolution timeout. Your internet router is failing to resolve your IPTV server domain, or your ISP is blocking access. Reboot your router or turn on a VPN.

Will clearing app data delete my configured playlists?

Yes. Clearing app data in Android settings resets the application to a fresh install. You will need to re-enter your Xtream Codes API login details.

What to Check Before You Subscribe Anywhere

A features list on a provider's homepage tells you what they claim, not what you'll actually get. Whatever service you're comparing, verify these points directly with the provider before paying for a full subscription:

Free trial or short test period

A provider confident in their service will usually let you test a stream before a full-length commitment.

Connection / device limits

Confirm how many simultaneous streams your plan actually includes — this is rarely on the homepage and often only shown at checkout.

Xtream Codes API vs. M3U-only

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Xtream Codes API, exactly?

It is a login method that gives an IPTV app three pieces of information &mdash; a server URL, username, and password &mdash; which the app then uses to automatically pull the full channel list, EPG, and VOD catalog, instead of manually loading a static playlist file.

Is Xtream Codes API better than an M3U link?

For most users, yes. Xtream Codes API typically loads faster, keeps your EPG synced automatically, and is easier to manage across multiple devices. See our full comparison in the M3U playlist guide linked below.

Can I use Xtream Codes credentials on more than one device?

That depends on your specific provider’s plan and connection limit, not on Xtream Codes itself. Check the provider’s stated device/connection limits before subscribing.
